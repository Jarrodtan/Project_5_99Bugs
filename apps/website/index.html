Keep the Himalayan valley in balance

An educational 3D ecosystem simulation for children aged 6–9. Set up grass, blue sheep and snow leopards, predict what will happen, run the simulation and discover how every living thing affects the others.

Download for Android See the features

How it works

BUILD → PREDICT → SIMULATE → OBSERVE → LEARN → ADJUST

Food chains are usually taught as static diagrams. EcoBalance lets young researchers change an ecosystem and watch the chain reaction unfold: too many snow leopards and the blue sheep disappear; too many sheep and the grass runs out.

Features

Free Exploration

Choose the starting animals and run your own ecosystem experiment.

Field Journal

Discover investigation cases unlocked by what actually happens in your valley.

Living animals

Blue sheep graze, flee and raise lambs; snow leopards hunt to survive.

Watch it change

A day counter and live population graph show the ecosystem rise and fall.

Real Himalayan data

Real-world weather from the Everest region feeds into the experience.

Guided Challenge (coming)

Make decisions with pros and cons and reach different endings.
